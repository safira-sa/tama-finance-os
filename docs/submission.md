# Submission narrative

## One-sentence pitch

Tama OS is a local-first deterministic decision-readiness layer that tells a personal investor what evidence or financial safeguard is missing before they take more risk.

## What is novel

Finance trackers explain what happened. Research tools explain an idea. Tama OS connects the two without silently merging or mutating either source: it checks whether the user has the financial readiness and decision-ready thesis required to act.

## Three-minute demo structure

1. **Problem (0:00–0:20):** “I own positions, but I do not know whether my finances and research are complete enough to add exposure.”
2. **Healthy baseline (0:20–0:50):** Import the Healthy Investor fixtures and show a ready-for-review brief.
3. **Decision gap (0:50–1:40):** Import Research Missing. Show BBCA covered while AMMN and BBRI are blocked because their theses are not decision-ready.
4. **Data-quality gap (1:40–2:15):** Import Chaotic User. Show high transaction volume without a monthly summary and missing Research.
5. **How it works (2:15–2:45):** Show the read-only snapshot → deterministic rules → evidence-backed recommendation flow and the smoke test.
6. **Why it is trustworthy (2:45–3:00):** Finance and Research remain independent; Tama OS makes no automatic writes, predictions, or black-box decisions.

## Claims to make

- Local-first and read-only at the OS layer.
- Deterministic recommendations are grounded in visible Finance and Research inputs.
- A holding is covered only by an active, intact, non-empty thesis.
- The project was built and hardened with Codex and GPT-5.6; provide the required Codex Session ID and dated development evidence in the Devpost form.

## Claims to avoid

- Financial advice, market prediction, live prices, or real-time market analysis.
- An in-product GPT chat or API integration, unless it is actually implemented and demonstrated.
- Saying a watchlist item is a complete thesis.
